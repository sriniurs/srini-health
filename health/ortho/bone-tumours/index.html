Bone Tumours & Lumps

Making sense of bone pain, lumps, scan findings, and tests used to investigate them.

  1. Night Knee Pain

    My teenager has knee pain at night. When should we worry?

    Why constant knee pain at night, swelling or a limp in a teenager needs an X-ray, how osteosarcoma is often missed in India, and when it really is just growing pains.

  2. Lump on The Bone

    I can feel a hard lump on my bone. What could it be?

    Why most hard lumps on bone are benign, such as osteochondroma, which features need a specialist, and why giant cell tumour near the knee is common in India.

  3. Soft-Tissue Lump

    Is my lump a harmless lipoma or something serious?

    Why most soft lumps are harmless lipomas, the red flags (over 5 cm, deep, growing, painful) that need a scan first, and why a sarcoma shouldn’t be “just removed”.

  4. Benign Bone Tumours

    The X-ray shows a cyst or benign tumour in my child’s bone. What now?

    Why simple bone cysts, osteochondromas and other benign tumours in children are common and often need only watching, and when treatment is needed.

  5. Bone Metastases

    Cancer has spread to my bones. Could the bone break?

    Why cancer that spreads to bone can weaken it, how doctors judge fracture risk, and when fixing the bone before it breaks helps.

  6. Biopsy

    Why should a bone tumour biopsy be done where it will be treated?

    Why a biopsy of a suspected bone or soft-tissue sarcoma should be planned by the team that will operate, and how a badly placed biopsy can cost a limb.
