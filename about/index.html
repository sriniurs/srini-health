About Health, Simply Told

A test result comes back higher than expected. Your shoulder hurts when you reach for a shelf. A doctor mentions knee replacement, and the questions come to you on the way home.

Health, Simply Told is an illustrated guide to questions like these. Each article begins with something a patient might ask and explains the terms, the tests and the decisions that can come up along the way.

What you’ll find here

General Health covers common symptoms, conditions and test results, from high blood pressure and blood sugar to headaches, dizziness and a cough that lingers.

Orthopaedics covers bones, joints and the spine. The collections on knee replacement, fractures, neck pain and shoulder problems explain what an assessment may involve, how treatment decisions are made and what recovery can look like.

Who’s behind it?

I’m Dr. Srini, based in Mysuru. I created this part of srini.presents to give patients and families a place to read at their own pace and find words for the questions they want to ask.

The articles draw on clinical guidelines, research and patient resources. You’ll find the sources at the end of each article so you can see where the information comes from.

Using these articles

These articles are for patient education. Use them to understand what you’ve been told and prepare questions for your appointment. For advice about your own symptoms, medicines or treatment, speak to a clinician who knows your situation.
