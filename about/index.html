About Health, Simply Told

Sometimes the questions come after the appointment. What does this test result mean? Why did my doctor suggest a scan? What should I ask at my next visit?

Health, Simply Told is an illustrated patient-education website from srini.presents. We start with the questions patients and families ask and explain symptoms, tests, treatment choices, and recovery in plain language. You can read at your own pace, revisit what you were told, or share an article with someone helping you make sense of it.

What you'll find here

  • General Health explains common symptoms, conditions, and test results.
  • Orthopaedics covers bones, joints, and the spine, from everyday pain and injuries to surgery and recovery.

If you're not sure where to start, use Search or browse the Article Index.

How we explain health

We draw on clinical guidelines, published research, and established patient resources. The sources listed at the end of an article let you see where the information comes from and read further.

We explain medical terms as they come up. Where there's more than one reasonable option, we explain why the choice may differ from person to person. Where the evidence is uncertain, we aim to say so. Stories used to illustrate a point are not accounts of real patients unless stated otherwise.

What this website can—and can't—do

Use these articles to understand what you've been told, prepare questions for an appointment, or talk through a decision with your clinician. The information is for patient education only. It cannot diagnose your symptoms, recommend treatment for you personally, or replace advice from a clinician who knows your situation. If you need urgent medical help, seek local medical care rather than emailing us.

Feedback and corrections

If something is unclear, a link is broken, or you think information is inaccurate or out of date, email srini.presents@gmail.com. Please include the article title or link and the section you're referring to. You're also welcome to suggest a topic.

Please don't send medical records or personal health details. This inbox is for feedback about the website, not for medical consultations.
